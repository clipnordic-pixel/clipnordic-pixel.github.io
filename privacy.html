Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy describes how clip-agent, a private tool operated by Clip Nordic, handles data.

Who uses the tool

clip-agent is used only by the owner of Clip Nordic, on their own computer, with their own YouTube channel and TikTok account. It has no other users.

What data we access

  • YouTube (Google): with the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly the tool uploads videos to our own channel and reads our own channel's name to confirm the right account is connected.
  • TikTok: with the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish the tool uploads videos to our own TikTok account.

The tool does not access, collect or store data about other users, viewers or commenters.

What we store

The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on our own computer, so the tool can upload without logging in every time. The links to uploaded videos are saved in a local log file for our own records. Nothing is sent to any other server than YouTube's and TikTok's official APIs.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Google API Services

clip-agent's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using YouTube API Services, the tool is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Deleting data and revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) and in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Apps and services. Revoking access and deleting the local token file removes all data the tool holds.
